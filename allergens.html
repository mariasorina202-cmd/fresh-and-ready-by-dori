ALLERGIES & INTOLERANCES

Food information you can use with confidence.

If you have a food allergy or intolerance, please tell our team before ordering. We will never guess allergen information.

Before we open

Our final allergen matrix will be completed from the exact recipes, final supplier products and labels used in the kitchen. Until that verification is complete, draft allergen information is not customer-facing.

Ordering online or for delivery

When online ordering goes live, allergen information will be available before purchase and again when food is delivered. Build Your Own customisations will need to reflect the ingredients selected in each order.

Cross-contact

Our final food-safety and allergen procedures will explain how we manage cross-contact risk in the actual premises and with the final equipment. Please speak to the team if you need this information before ordering.

Contact us

Questions before ordering? Email freshandreadybydori@gmail.com.
